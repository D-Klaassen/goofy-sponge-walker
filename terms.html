Playing for free

The game is free to play. Steps and Step outfits are saved in your browser and need no account.

Coins

Coins are a virtual currency for Premium items in the game. You buy them in packs through Stripe; prices are in euros and include 21% Dutch VAT. Coins are linked to your account (the email address you sign in with), have no cash value, cannot be transferred and are only used inside this game.

Your right of withdrawal

Coins are delivered right after payment. At checkout you agree to this and confirm that you lose your legal 14-day right of withdrawal once the Coins are delivered.

As a goodwill gesture, you can still withdraw a Coin pack within 14 days of buying it, as long as none of its Coins have been spent. Use the Withdraw button in the shop; you get the full price back on your original payment method and the pack's Coins are removed. Coins are spent from your oldest pack first, and Coins you get back from a Return are spent before any pack, so your newest pack stays withdrawable as long as possible.

Returning Premium items

You can return a Premium item at any time and get 80% of its Coin price back as Coins, rounded up. Returned Coins are not money and cannot be withdrawn. You can buy a returned item again later at its full price.

Chargebacks and refunds

If a payment is reversed by your bank or refunded in full, the Coins from that pack are removed, even if your balance goes negative. You keep the items you already own, but you cannot buy anything until your balance is positive again.

Changes and availability

The game is a hobby project and is offered as is. We may change the game, the items or these terms; changes never take away Coins or items you already have. If we ever stop offering Coins, we will tell players in the game beforehand.

Privacy

We store your email address and your Coin history to run your account. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card or bank details. Ask us by email to see or delete your data.
