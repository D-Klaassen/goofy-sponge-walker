# Withdrawal is waived at checkout, then offered back as goodwill

EU law (Directive 2011/83/EU art. 16(m), Dutch BW 6:230p) gives consumers 14 days to withdraw from an online purchase, but for digital content that right ends at delivery if the buyer ticks an explicit consent-and-acknowledgement box and the confirmation email repeats it. We take that waiver, so a Player can't buy Coins, spend them and still claim the money back. On top of it we voluntarily refund a Coin pack in full within 14 days while none of its Coins are spent (oldest pack is spent first).

## Considered options

- **Waiver only**: legal, but harsher than needed for untouched packs.
- **Full 14-day withdrawal, no waiver**: lets Players spend Coins and still get refunded.

## Consequences

- The balance must track which pack each spent Coin came from.
- Item Returns (80% back as Coins) are a separate, purely in-game rule and never give money back.
